seeing.eye/more

Seeing.eye is a group of cybersecurity enthusiasts. Is there a place for beginners? Absolutely! We’ll gladly train newcomers, and we can tackle CTF (Capture The Flag) challenges together. On our Discord server, we dive into penetration testing, reverse engineering, and OSINT, while also participating in team-based CTF competitions. You can also help our community grow—share the link with a friend so they can join us in exploring cybersecurity after school, work, or college! Our server features a responsive moderation team ready to assist you if you run into any issues. If you’d prefer not to reach out to the moderators, you can message the server admin directly—they’re almost always online.
